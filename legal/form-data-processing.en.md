### Data processing

With your consent, {{association.short_name}} stores your details and signature as the
attendance list of this event, to document who took part and to prove it to the bodies that
fund our events. Only MESA organisers can see them. They are deleted at the end of the
{{retention.years}}th year after the event, or earlier if you withdraw your consent.

Consenting is voluntary: if you prefer not to, ask the organisers to note your attendance on
paper instead. You can withdraw your consent at any time by emailing <{{dpo.email}}>; your
entry is then deleted.
