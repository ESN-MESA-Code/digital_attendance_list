### Data processing

{{association.short_name}} stores your details and signature as the attendance list of this
event, to document who took part and to prove it to the bodies that fund our events. Only
MESA organisers can see them. They are deleted at the end of the {{retention.years}}th year
after the event.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in proving that funded events took place and who
attended (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), and your acceptance of the disclaimer (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
No law obliges you to sign in; if you prefer not to sign in here, ask the organisers for a
paper list. You can object to the processing at any time by emailing <{{association.email}}>.
