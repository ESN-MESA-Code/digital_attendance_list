## 2. Signing in to an event

The app is used for MESA's regular events. Funded events such as trips keep a paper
attendance list, which our general privacy notice covers.

**What we store:** first and last name, the time of signing in, whether you signed in on your
own device or on an organiser's (and whose), and which version of the participation terms and
of this information you were shown.

**What we only count:** if you answer the optional questions (nationality, university,
exchange programme, whether you have an ESNcard), we add your answers to the event's counts
(for example “Italy: 12”). They are never stored with your name or your sign-in.

**Why and on what legal basis:**

- To prove that you accepted the participation terms for the event, and to defend against
  legal claims that could arise from it. Legal basis: the agreement on your participation
  (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and our legitimate interest in being able to prove what was agreed
  (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
- To show our finance team that an event took place and how many people came, so the organisers
  get their expenses back. Only counts are used for this, no names. Legal basis: our
  legitimate interest in proper accounts (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
- Statistics without names for planning our events and for reports, from the counts of the
  optional answers. They are shown once the list is closed (at the sign-off, or 24 hours
  after the event). Legal basis: our legitimate interest in planning our events (Art. 6(1)(f)
  GDPR).

**Do you have to sign in?** No law obliges you to. If you do not want to sign in digitally,
ask the organisers for a paper list. The optional questions can be left out.

**Who sees it:** MESA organisers with their @esnmesa.org account see the names while the event
is on (from 2 hours before it until the sign-off, at the latest 24 hours after it). After
that only our executives see the names. Organisers and our finance team see the counts.

**How long:** deleted automatically at the end of the {{retention.ordinal_en}} calendar year
after the event (for an event in {{retention.example_event_year}}: at the end of
{{retention.example_deletion_year}}) – the period in which claims from the event could be
raised. The event's counts are deleted with it. If something happens at an event, we keep that event's list until the matter is
settled.

## 3. “Save my details” on your device

If you click “Save my details” after signing in, your name and any optional answers you gave
are saved in your browser's storage. They stay on your device, are never sent to our servers
and are forgotten after 12 months. Legal basis: your consent (§ 25(1) TDDDG, Art. 6(1)(a)
GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time with “Not you? Remove saved details” or “Remove them”
in the form, or by deleting this site's data in your browser.

## 4. Organiser login

Organisers sign in with their @esnmesa.org Google account ({{processors.login}}). We receive
their name, email address and account ID. At each login, and once a day, the app asks Google
whether the organiser belongs to the executives' group, and stores the email addresses of the
group's members. The app records which organiser created, recorded sign-ins for and signed off
(with the number of people counted) each list; this is kept as long as the event. It also
records when an organiser accepted the confidentiality rules for organisers, kept until the
end of the fifth calendar year after acceptance. The login service records the IP address and
browser of each login for security; we delete these after 30 days. Legal basis: our
legitimate interest in secure access and in knowing who is responsible for each list (Art.
6(1)(f) GDPR). The login cookies are strictly necessary and need no consent (§ 25(2) No. 2
TDDDG). Participants get no cookies.

## 5. Hosting and service providers

The app is hosted by {{processors.hosting}} on servers in the EU; the database is run by
{{processors.database}} in Frankfurt am Main. When you open the app, they process your IP
address, the time, the page requested and your browser to deliver the app and keep it secure,
and keep this only briefly in their logs (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Vercel, Supabase and Google act on our behalf under data processing agreements (Art. 28
GDPR). They or their subcontractors may access data from outside the EU, in particular the
USA. Such transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the company is certified,
otherwise on the EU standard contractual clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR).

## 6. Your rights

You have the right to access your data (Art. 15 GDPR), to have it corrected (Art. 16),
deleted (Art. 17) or its processing restricted (Art. 18), to receive your name and the record
of accepting the participation terms in a machine-readable format (Art. 20), and to withdraw a
consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)).

**Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR): you can object at any time, on grounds relating to your
particular situation, to processing based on our legitimate interest (sections 2, 4 and 5).
We then stop unless we have compelling legitimate grounds or need the data for legal claims –
for example after an incident at the event you attended; we then tell you so.**
