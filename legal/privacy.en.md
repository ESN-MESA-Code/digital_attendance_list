## 2. Signing in to an event

**What we store:** first and last name, nationality, university, exchange programme, whether
you have an ESNcard, your handwritten signature (as a small image), the time of signing in,
whether you signed in on your own device or on an organiser's (and whose), and which version
of this information you were shown.

**Why and on what legal basis:**

- As a signed attendance list that proves to the bodies funding our association (for example
  “Lokale Erasmus+ Initiativen”) and to our accounting that our events took place and who
  took part, and for statistics without names for funder reports and event planning. Legal
  basis: our legitimate interest in keeping this funding (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The funders'
  attendance-list template asks for each participant's nationality, university, exchange
  programme and ESNcard status.
- Your signature also confirms that you accept the event's liability disclaimer. Legal basis:
  the agreement on your participation (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

**Do you have to sign in?** No law obliges you to. If you do not want to sign in digitally,
ask the organisers for a paper list.

**Who sees it:** only MESA organisers with their @esnmesa.org account; a printed copy is kept
in our office. Funders or auditors may see the lists when they check how funds were used.

**How long:** deleted automatically at the end of the {{retention.years}}th calendar year after the event (for an
event in {{retention.example_event_year}}: at the end of {{retention.example_deletion_year}}),
printed lists at the same time.

## 3. “Save my details” on your device

If you click “Save my details” after signing in, your name, nationality, university, exchange
programme and ESNcard status are saved in your browser's storage – never your signature. They
stay on your device, are never sent to our servers and are forgotten after 12 months. Legal
basis: your consent (§ 25(1) TDDDG, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time with
“Not you? Remove saved details” or “Remove them” in the form, or by deleting this site's data
in your browser.

## 4. Organiser login

Organisers sign in with their @esnmesa.org Google account ({{processors.login}}). We receive
their name, email address, profile picture and account ID; the profile picture is loaded
from Google, which sees the organiser's IP address. The app records which organiser created,
recorded sign-ins for, signed off (with their signature) and filed each list; this is kept
as long as the event. It also records when an organiser accepted the confidentiality rules for
organisers, kept until the end of the fifth calendar year after acceptance. The login service records the IP address and browser of each login for
security; we delete these after 30 days. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in secure
access and in knowing who is responsible for each list (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The login cookies
are strictly necessary and need no consent (§ 25(2) No. 2 TDDDG). Participants get no
cookies.

## 5. Hosting and service providers

The app is hosted by {{processors.hosting}} on servers in the EU; the database is run by
{{processors.database}} in Frankfurt am Main. When you open the app, they process your IP
address, the time, the page requested and your browser to deliver the app and keep it secure,
and keep this only briefly in their logs (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Vercel, Supabase and Google act on our behalf under data processing agreements (Art. 28
GDPR). They or their subcontractors may access data from outside the EU, in particular the
USA. Such transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the company is certified,
otherwise on the EU standard contractual clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR).

## 6. Your rights

You have the right to access your data (Art. 15 GDPR), to have it corrected (Art. 16),
deleted (Art. 17) or its processing restricted (Art. 18), to receive your name and signature,
given to accept the disclaimer, in a machine-readable format (Art. 20), and to withdraw a
consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)).

**Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR): you can object at any time, on grounds relating to your
particular situation, to processing based on our legitimate interest (sections 2, 4 and 5).
We then stop unless we have compelling legitimate grounds or need the data for legal claims.
For an attendance list this can be the case until we have reported the year's events to our
funders; we then tell you so and delete your entry once that report is done.**
