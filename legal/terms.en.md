# Participation terms

{{association.name}} ("MESA") · version {{terms_version}}

These terms apply when you take part in a regular MESA event. You accept them when you sign
in to the event's attendance list. Trips and other events with their own registration have
their own terms.

## 1. Who may take part

1. Participants must be at least 18 years old.
2. You decide yourself whether your health and fitness allow you to take part in an activity.
   If in doubt, ask a doctor beforehand.

## 2. Conduct

1. Follow the instructions of the organisers and of the venue or other providers (for example
   guides or staff).
2. Only take part in activities that match your abilities, and use the safety equipment that
   is required or provided.
3. Treat other participants, organisers and everyone else with respect. Harassment,
   discrimination and violence are not tolerated.
4. Organisers may exclude anyone under the influence of alcohol or drugs from an activity.

## 3. Exclusion

1. MESA may exclude participants from all or part of an event if they seriously or repeatedly
   break these terms or the organisers' instructions, or endanger themselves or others.
2. If a fee was paid, MESA keeps it minus any expenses it saves.

## 4. Risks of the activity

Some activities carry risks of their own, such as falls in sports or changing weather and
terrain on hikes. Careful organisation cannot remove them entirely. Where an event has
particular risks, the organisers describe them in a risk notice shown when you sign in; it is
part of these terms for that event.

## 5. Liability of MESA

1. MESA is liable without limitation
   - for intent and gross negligence,
   - for injury to life, body or health,
   - where MESA has given a guarantee,
   - under the Product Liability Act and other mandatory statutory provisions.
2. In all other cases of slight negligence, MESA is liable only for breach of essential
   obligations: obligations whose fulfilment makes the proper running of the event possible in
   the first place, and on which participants regularly rely. In such cases, liability is
   limited to damage that is typical for this kind of event and foreseeable when you signed in.
3. Apart from this, MESA's liability for slight negligence is excluded.
4. These provisions also apply to the personal liability of MESA's board members, members,
   volunteers and other agents.
5. MESA does not look after participants' belongings unless this has been expressly agreed.

## 6. Venues and other providers

Where an event takes place at or with someone else (for example a bar, museum, sports venue or
tour provider), their own terms and house rules apply to their services. MESA remains liable
for its own obligations, such as choosing them with care.

## 7. Liability of participants

Participants are liable under the statutory provisions for damage they cause to MESA, other
participants or third parties.
